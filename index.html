Our Philosophy

Two traditions,
one breath

Komorebi emerges at the quiet intersection where Scandinavian warmth meets Japanese contemplation—a space where the unhurried precision of wabi-sabi and the embracing comfort of hygge become one practice.

We believe the modern mind craves not more stimulation, but more intentional stillness. Our dawn retreats guide you through the ancient rhythms of tea ceremony, forest bathing, and mindful craft, set within spaces designed to quiet the noise and amplify the senses.

Each morning begins before the world fully wakes—when light is softest, air is cleanest, and the boundary between self and nature dissolves into something simpler.

Our Rituals

Three paths to morning stillness

Each ritual is a threshold—a gentle passage from the noise of daily life into something more deliberate, more present, more yourself.

Cha no Yu

A guided tea ceremony at dawn—not instruction, but immersion. Learn to pour with presence, steep with patience, and drink with your whole being. The bowl in your hands becomes a world.

90 minutes · sunrise

Shinrin-yoku

Forest bathing through ancient birch and cedar groves. No destination, no distance—only the practice of being fully arrived. Let the canopy filter both light and thought.

2 hours · pre-dawn

Te no Shigoto

Hands-on craft workshops in stoneware ceramics and Nordic woodworking. Shape raw clay, join timber without nails, and discover the quiet satisfaction of making something with honest imperfection.

Half day · morning light

Craftsmanship

Made by hand,
measured in attention

Every element of a Komorebi retreat is touched by human hands. Our tea bowls are thrown by local ceramicists who understand that a slight unevenness in the lip makes each sip more intimate. Our retreat pavilions are built using traditional timber joinery—no screws, no adhesive—just wood understanding wood.

We source materials the way a good poem sources words: only what is essential, only what earns its place. Undyed linen. Unfired clay. Unfinished cedar. Raw materials that age honestly and gain character, not obsolescence.

Elimination of clutter, stripped to essential beauty

Seasonal Moments

Each season holds
a different silence

Our retreats change with the light. What you experience in the amber of autumn differs profoundly from the clear stillness of a winter dawn.

Autumn · 秋

Amber Settling

Persimmon tea under maple canopy, as leaves perform their final choreography

Spring · 春

First Unfurling

New growth meditation walks, shinrin-yoku through birch stands when sap rises and air shimmers

Summer · 夏

Long Light

Extended evening ceremonies where twilight stretches and time softens into warm amber stillness

Winter · 冬

Bare Clarity

Snow-silence workshops in ceramic and wood. When the world empties, the hands fill with purpose
